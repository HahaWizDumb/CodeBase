Chaos Magic & the Meditation on Renewal Cycles:
Hopi Past Worlds Meet Egyptian Primordial Waters

In chaos magic, belief is the only tool. Sigils, gnosis, and paradigm shifting become the obsidian blade that cuts through consensus reality. What better paradigm than the eternal cycle of destruction and rebirth?

The Hopi elders speak of four previous worlds. The First was destroyed by fire. The Second by ice. The Third by flood. The Fourth ended in war and greed — each time Maasaw, the skeleton fire god, appeared to guide the righteous into the next. The current Fifth World trembles again.

Across the Nile, Egyptian cosmology begins in Nun — the dark, chaotic waters that birthed and will one day swallow all. Atum-Ra creates, Sekhmet destroys in rage, and the world is renewed. Zep Tepi, the First Time, is not a singular event but a recurring possibility. Chaos magicians meditate here: collapse your current paradigm into the floodwaters of Nun or the Hopi fire. Let it burn. Let it drown. Then sigil the next world into being.

“The frog laughs in the primordial slime. The skeleton grins at the edge of the cliff. Both know the joke: the end is the beginning.” — Anonymous chaos magician, 2025

Practical meditation: Sit in darkness. Visualize Kali dancing on Shiva while Christ bleeds on the cross. Feel the Kek frog and Maasaw skeleton laughing together as the Hopi Fourth World sinks and the Egyptian waters rise. Breathe the cycle. When the tension peaks, release your intention as a living sigil. The ground is fertile.
